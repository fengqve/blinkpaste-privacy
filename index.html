秒贴支持与隐私政策

秒贴是一款 iPhone 键盘，用来保存常用文本、话术、地址、签名和 AI 提示词，并在任意输入框里快速贴出。

中文支持

如何开始

  1. 打开秒贴，保存或新建一条片段。
  2. 打开 iOS 设置 > 通用 > 键盘 > 键盘。
  3. 添加“秒贴”键盘。
  4. 打开“允许完全访问”，让键盘读取你在主 App 里保存的片段。
  5. 回到任意输入框，切到秒贴，点卡片即可贴上。

为什么需要“允许完全访问”？

iOS 自定义键盘需要这个权限，才能读取主 App 共享容器里的片段库。秒贴只读取你保存的片段，不记录你在其他 App 中输入的内容。

秒贴会上传我的输入内容吗？

不会。秒贴键盘不记录也不上传你在其他 App 中输入的内容。

iCloud 同步会同步什么？

如果设备已登录 iCloud，秒贴会通过 Apple 的 iCloud 能力同步你保存的片段和相关设置，方便你在自己的设备间使用。

免费版有什么限制？

前 30 条片段可免费使用。继续添加更多片段时，可以通过一次性买断解锁无限片段。

中文隐私政策

生效日期：2026-06-30

秒贴尊重你的隐私。秒贴的目标是让你保存过的常用文本更快出现，而不是记录你的输入。

我们处理的信息

秒贴会在你的设备上保存以下内容：

  • 你主动保存的片段、标题、正文和分类信息
  • 片段排序、置顶、收藏、使用次数等本地设置
  • 是否解锁 Pro 的购买状态

如果你开启 iCloud 并登录同一 Apple ID，秒贴会使用 Apple 的 iCloud 能力在你的设备之间同步片段和相关设置。

我们不收集的信息

秒贴不会收集、记录或上传：

  • 你在其他 App 中输入的内容
  • 键盘按键记录
  • 联系人
  • 位置信息
  • 浏览记录
  • 用于跨 App 追踪的标识符

键盘完全访问

iOS 会要求自定义键盘开启“允许完全访问”，才能读取主 App 共享容器里的内容。秒贴使用该权限读取你保存的片段库。秒贴不会用它记录你的键入内容。

购买

秒贴 Pro 通过 Apple App Store / StoreKit 处理。购买确认、退款和付款信息由 Apple 处理。秒贴只读取必要的购买状态，用于判断是否解锁无限片段。

删除数据

你可以在 App 中删除已保存片段。删除 App 会移除设备上的本地数据；如果你开启了 iCloud，同步数据的保留和删除还受 Apple iCloud 机制影响。

English Support

BlinkPaste is an iPhone keyboard for saving reusable text, replies, addresses, signatures, and AI prompts so you can paste them quickly from any text field.

Getting Started

  1. Open BlinkPaste and save or create a snippet.
  2. Open iOS Settings > General > Keyboard > Keyboards.
  3. Add the BlinkPaste keyboard.
  4. Turn on Allow Full Access so the keyboard can read snippets saved in the main app.
  5. Open any text field, switch to BlinkPaste, and tap a card to paste.

Why does BlinkPaste need Allow Full Access?

iOS custom keyboards need this permission to read data from the app's shared container. BlinkPaste uses it to read your saved snippet library. It does not record what you type in other apps.

Does BlinkPaste upload my typing content?

No. BlinkPaste does not record or upload what you type in other apps.

What syncs with iCloud?

If your device is signed in to iCloud, BlinkPaste uses Apple's iCloud features to sync saved snippets and related settings across your own devices.

What is included for free?

The first 30 snippets are free. You can unlock unlimited snippets with a one-time purchase.

English Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-06-30

BlinkPaste respects your privacy. The app is designed to make your saved reusable text easier to paste, not to record your typing.

Information Processed

BlinkPaste stores the following data on your device:

  • Snippets you create or save, including titles, body text, and categories
  • Local settings such as sorting, pinned snippets, favorites, and usage counts
  • Whether BlinkPaste Pro is unlocked

If you use iCloud with the same Apple ID, BlinkPaste uses Apple's iCloud features to sync saved snippets and related settings across your own devices.

Information Not Collected

BlinkPaste does not collect, record, or upload:

  • What you type in other apps
  • Keystroke logs
  • Contacts
  • Location
  • Browsing history
  • Identifiers for cross-app tracking

Keyboard Full Access

iOS requires custom keyboards to use Allow Full Access before they can read content from the app's shared container. BlinkPaste uses this permission to read your saved snippet library. BlinkPaste does not use it to record your typed content.

Purchases

BlinkPaste Pro purchases are handled by Apple App Store / StoreKit. Payment details, confirmations, and refunds are handled by Apple. BlinkPaste reads the minimum purchase state needed to unlock unlimited snippets.

Data Deletion

You can delete saved snippets in the app. Deleting the app removes local data from the device. If iCloud sync is enabled, synced data retention and deletion may also depend on Apple's iCloud behavior.
